A portrait made for the place they hold in your home.

Turn one favorite pet photo into a custom portrait, professionally printed and set in a solid-wood frame. Choose the size, wood finish, and presentation that feels right for your space.

How it works

  1. Choose your frame. Select a size, wood finish, and mat style.
  2. Upload one clear photo. Add your pet’s name and a well-lit reference image.
  3. Review the artwork. We prepare your portrait and send a digital proof before printing.
  4. We print, frame, and ship. Production begins after approval, and tracking is emailed when your order leaves us.

Choose your presentation

No Mat places the portrait edge to edge inside the frame opening for a bold, modern look.

Classic White Mat uses a real, separately cut white mat board—not a printed white border. The board is approximately 1 mm thick, so its cut window adds a subtle paper edge and a very light natural shadow without looking bulky.

For the best portrait

  • Upload a sharp, well-lit photo at the highest available resolution.
  • Make sure the eyes, face, ears, and natural markings are clearly visible.
  • Avoid screenshots, heavy filters, low light, or photos where part of the face is covered.
  • This listing includes one pet. Contact us before ordering if you need more than one pet in the same portrait.
